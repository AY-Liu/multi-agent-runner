# The Missing Birthday Cake

## 1. One-Paragraph Game Concept

The birthday cake has vanished 30 minutes before candles, leaving only a smug note from the "cake protector." A team of 6-8 friends follows three simple paper-and-phone clues around one room to prove they are worthy of dessert. No locks are needed: each answer points to the next hiding place, and the final clue reveals the cake, snack, or paper "Cake Found" note.

## 2. 30-Minute Run Of Show

- 0-3 min: Host reads intro, starts phone timer, gives Envelope 1.
- 3-10 min: Players solve the birthday card acrostic and search the fridge/freezer area.
- 10-17 min: Players use the phone photo clue to get the word `CAKES` and find Envelope 3 near the cake-serving area.
- 17-25 min: Players count visible party items to get final code `432`.
- 25-28 min: Host accepts the code and directs them to the final hiding place.
- 28-30 min: Reveal cake/snack/note and read the closing line.

## 3. Puzzle List With Answers And Hints

1. Birthday Card Acrostic: Write six birthday-card lines whose first letters spell `FRIDGE`. Answer: `FRIDGE`, leading to Envelope 2 taped near the fridge/freezer. Hint: "Look at the start of every line."
2. Phone Photo Clue: On a phone, show a photo or note listing objects: cup, apple, key, envelope, spoon. First letters spell `CAKES`, leading to Envelope 3 near plates, napkins, or the cake-serving spot. Hint: "Name the objects in order."
3. Room Object Count: Envelope 3 says, "Count the party things: candles + balloons + plates." Use drawn or real visible items, e.g. 4 candles, 3 balloons, 2 plates. Answer: `432`. Hint: "Count in the order written."

## 4. Story / Host Script

Start: "The cake has vanished 30 minutes before candles. This note says: 'I hid the cake to protect it from early snackers. Prove you are worthy. Follow the crumbs.'"

If stuck: "The clue is literal. It points to something in this room."

After Puzzle 1: "Cold things nap nearby."

After Puzzle 2: "That word belongs wherever cake is served."

After Puzzle 3: "Correct. The cake protector accepts your code."

End: "Cake recovered. Birthday saved. Candles may proceed."

## 5. Setup Checklist

- Prepare 3 envelopes labeled `1`, `2`, and `3`, plus a final `Cake Found` note.
- Put Envelope 1 in plain sight or hand it to players.
- Tape Envelope 2 near the fridge/freezer area.
- Put Envelope 3 near plates, napkins, or a serving surface.
- Place 4 candle drawings/items, 3 balloon drawings/items, and 2 plates visibly in the room.
- Hide the final cake/snack/note in a cupboard, drawer, fridge, or under a clean towel.
- Keep one phone available for the timer and one phone/photo/note for Puzzle 2.

## 6. Risks Or Conflicts Found During Review

- Resolved conflict: operations suggested 6 envelopes, while the puzzle path only needs 3 plus a final reveal. Final version uses 3 envelopes to stay easy.
- Resolved conflict: story suggested a confusing word puzzle. Final version uses the simpler acrostic, photo, and counting puzzles.
- Risk: players may overthink clues. Give a hint after 4 minutes stuck: first point to the area, then the object, then explain the puzzle step.
- Fallback: at 20 minutes, skip to the next envelope; at 27 minutes, point directly toward the final hiding area.
