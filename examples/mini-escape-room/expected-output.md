# Expected Output Shape

The provider output may vary, but a successful demo should produce `output/mini_escape_room_demo.md` with:

- A short concept for "The Missing Birthday Cake"
- A 30-minute timeline
- Three puzzles with answers and hints
- Host script or story beats
- Setup checklist using only common items
- Notes about resolved conflicts or risks

The important behavior is the workflow:

1. The leader launches three groups in parallel.
2. Each group performs a small internal workflow.
3. The leader waits for group outputs.
4. The leader synthesizes one final plan.
